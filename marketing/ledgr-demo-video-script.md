# Ledgr — Demo Video: Shooting Script & Shot List

**Product:** Ledgr — *Know Your Numbers. Run Your Business.*
**Goal:** Show a Nigerian SME owner that Ledgr turns simple transaction entry into
professional financial reports — in seconds.
**Primary cut:** ~90 seconds (landing page / YouTube / WhatsApp).
**Social cut:** ~30 seconds (Instagram / X / LinkedIn) — see end.
**Tone:** Warm, confident, plain-spoken. Not "accounting software" — a financial
co-pilot for busy founders.

---

## Pre-production checklist (5 min)

- **Record on the live app:** `https://www.ledgr.com.ng`
- **Log in as the demo business:** `demo@ledgr.test` (this is **Tayo Foods Limited**,
  which has the rich demo data — ₦3.7m cash, revenue, invoices, full reports).
- **Browser:** Chrome, window ≥ 1440×900, **hide the bookmarks bar**, close other tabs,
  zoom at 100%. Use an incognito window for a clean chrome.
- **Cursor:** enable click-highlight (ScreenStudio/Loom do this) so taps are visible.
- **Recorder:** ScreenStudio, Loom, or OBS. Record at 1080p, 30fps.
- **Also grab a mobile pass:** resize the window to ~400px wide (or use device toolbar)
  to capture the mobile layout for Scene 6.
- **Music:** upbeat, light Afrobeat/lo-fi bed at ~15% under the voice.

> Tip: record each scene as its own clip so re-takes are easy, then assemble.

## The 90-second script

Format — **VO** = voiceover (read this). **SCREEN** = what to do on camera.
**CAPTION** = on-screen text/lower-third.

### Scene 1 — The hook (0:00–0:10)
- **SCREEN:** Open on the **marketing homepage** (`ledgr.com.ng`), then cut to the
  **Dashboard** for Tayo Foods Limited.
- **VO:** "Running a business, you only really want to know four things: Did I make
  money? Where did it go? Who owes me? And what do I owe?"
- **CAPTION:** *Ledgr — Know Your Numbers. Run Your Business.*

### Scene 2 — The dashboard answers everything (0:10–0:25)
- **SCREEN:** Slow pan across the **Dashboard** headline cards → **Cash Balance
  ₦3,700,000**, **Revenue**, **Expenses**, **Net Profit ₦800,000**. Then hover the
  **"How your business is doing"** insights card.
- **VO:** "Ledgr answers all four the second you open it. Your cash, your profit,
  who owes you — and a plain-English summary of how the month is going. No
  spreadsheets, no accounting degree."
- **CAPTION:** *Your whole business, in one glance.*

### Scene 3 — Recording is stupidly simple (0:25–0:42)
- **SCREEN:** Click **Record Transaction**. Pick **"Money Received."** Type an amount
  (e.g. **450,000**), choose **GTBank**, category **Sales**, description "Catering
  event." Click **Save**. Cut to the transaction appearing in the feed.
- **VO:** "Recording money is this simple. Say what happened in plain language —
  money in, money out — and Ledgr does the double-entry accounting for you, correctly,
  in the background. Every time."
- **CAPTION:** *You enter it. Ledgr does the accounting.*

### Scene 4 — Invoicing & getting paid (0:42–0:58)
- **SCREEN:** Go to **Sales & Invoices** → open **INV-0001** (₦537,500, 7.5% VAT).
  Show the line items and totals. Click **Record Payment**, enter a part-payment,
  save → status flips to **Part-paid**, **Outstanding** updates.
- **VO:** "Send an invoice, add VAT, take a payment — and Ledgr automatically tracks
  exactly who still owes you, and how much. Your receivables update themselves."
- **CAPTION:** *Invoices, VAT, and receivables — handled.*

### Scene 5 — The payoff: real financial reports (0:58–1:18)
- **SCREEN:** Go to **Reports.** Click through, pausing ~2s on each:
  **Profit & Loss** (Net Profit ₦800,000) → **Balance Sheet** (show the green
  **"Balanced"** badge, Assets = Liabilities + Equity) → **Cash Flow** → then open
  the **Management Account** and click **PDF** to reveal the board-ready document.
- **VO:** "And here's the magic. From those simple entries, Ledgr builds real
  management accounts — Profit and Loss, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow — every figure
  tracing back to the same ledger, always in balance. One click gives you a
  board-ready PDF to send your bank, investors, or partners."
- **CAPTION:** *P&L · Balance Sheet · Cash Flow · Management Accounts — automatically.*

### Scene 6 — Runs from your phone (1:18–1:28)
- **SCREEN:** Switch to the **mobile view** — bottom nav + the floating **+** button.
  Tap **+**, show the quick Record Transaction sheet.
- **VO:** "It all runs from your phone — so you can keep your books straight from
  anywhere, between customers."
- **CAPTION:** *Built mobile-first.*

### Scene 7 — Close & CTA (1:28–1:35)
- **SCREEN:** Cut back to the Dashboard, then a clean end card with the logo + URL.
- **VO:** "Ledgr. Know your numbers, run your business. Start free at ledgr.com.ng —
  three thousand naira a month, everything included."
- **CAPTION:** *Start free → ledgr.com.ng · ₦3,000/mo*

## Do / Don't
- **Do** keep the cursor slow and deliberate; let each report breathe for ~2 seconds.
- **Do** show the "Balanced" / "Active" badges — they signal real accounting + trust.
- **Don't** show the platform **/admin** area in the customer-facing cut (that's the
  operator console — reserve it for investor decks).
- **Don't** rush Scene 5 — the auto-generated reports are the whole pitch.
